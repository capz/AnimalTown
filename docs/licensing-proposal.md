# Proposed AnimalTown distribution terms

This is a draft for the owner's review. It grants no permission and does not
replace existing licenses. The release and registry tooling must not treat it
as an approved license.

The current DolphinDotNet and Hypercube dependencies declare GPL-2.0-only.
The restrictions below cannot override those licenses and generally cannot
apply to redistribution of a linked game without valid alternative licensing
from the relevant rights holders. They could separately govern original art
where the granting owner has the necessary rights. Resolve this before adopting
terms or submitting a hosted Depot entry.

## Proposed grant for original AnimalTown work

The copyright holders permit individuals to download, run and study AnimalTown
for personal, noncommercial purposes and to make private modifications for those
purposes. Copyright and attribution notices must be retained.

Public redistribution, publication of modified versions, commercial use, sale,
sublicensing and inclusion in another distributed product require prior written
permission from the relevant copyright holders. Permissions granted by the
hosting platform's terms, and rights that cannot legally be restricted, remain
unaffected. Ownership of the original work does not transfer to recipients.

These are source-available terms, not an open-source license. They apply only to
original work the granting copyright holders are entitled to license. Third-party
components and assets retain their own licenses and notices; those terms are
not replaced or restricted by this grant. A component and asset inventory must
identify any material needing separate permission.

## Optional Dolphin Depot permission

If separately approved by the rights holders, Dolphin Depot may copy, host,
cache and redistribute official, unmodified AnimalTown release packages through
its registry and CDN for free installation and updates. Required copyright,
license and third-party notices must accompany the packages. This permission
does not authorize resale, modified releases, branding changes or other
commercial use, and does not cover third-party material beyond its existing
terms or separately documented permission.

The final terms still need the granting rights holders' names, the covered code
and assets, the permission contact, and the owner's decision on the Depot grant.
